# AllCalcKit

> One website for every calculation and conversion you need daily.

Free online toolkit for unit conversion, BMI, EMI, currency, percentage, date, and 50+ daily calculators — no ads, no login, no clutter.

---

## What is AllCalcKit?

AllCalcKit is a single, clean website that replaces all those scattered, ad-heavy calculator sites you bookmark separately. Everything you need for daily calculations and conversions lives in one place — from converting kilometers to miles, to calculating your EMI, to tracking your BMI.

---

## Features

### Unit Converters
Length, Weight, Temperature, Volume, Area, Speed, Data Storage, Pressure, Energy, Time, Angle, Fuel Efficiency

### Currency & Finance
Currency Converter (live rates), EMI / Loan Calculator, SIP Calculator, FD / RD Calculator, Compound & Simple Interest, GST / Tax Calculator, Tip Calculator, Discount Calculator, Profit & Loss, Salary / CTC Calculator

### Health & Fitness
BMI, BMR, TDEE, Body Fat %, Ideal Body Weight, Calorie Intake Planner, Water Intake, Pregnancy Due Date, Ovulation Calculator, Age Calculator

### Math & Numbers
Percentage Calculator, Fraction ↔ Decimal, Number Base Converter, Scientific Calculator, Roman Numerals, Prime Checker, LCM / GCD, Ratio & Proportion, Mean / Median / Mode, Standard Deviation

### Date & Time
Date Difference, Date Add / Subtract, Time Zone Converter, Stopwatch / Timer, Unix Timestamp, Week Number, Working Days Calculator

### Cooking & Food
Recipe Converter, Cooking Measurements, Oven Temperature, Nutritional Macro Calculator

### Construction & Everyday Life
Paint Calculator, Flooring / Tile Calculator, Concrete Mix Calculator, Electricity Bill Estimator, Fuel Cost Calculator, Clothing Size Converter

### Text & Encoding
Word / Character Counter, Color Converter (HEX ↔ RGB ↔ HSL), Base64 Encoder/Decoder, URL Encoder/Decoder, Case Converter

---

## Design Philosophy

- **No ads** — distraction-free experience
- **No login** — everything works without an account
- **Mobile-first** — fully usable on a phone with one hand
- **Instant results** — no loading spinners for simple calculations
- **Offline-capable** — core tools work without internet (PWA)
- **Shareable URLs** — every calculator state is bookmarkable and shareable
